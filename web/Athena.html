Athena Superpack Pvt. Ltd. is a disposable food-packaging company. Under our brand Zapac, we supply food businesses, resellers, corporate customers, hotels, restaurants, cloud kitchens and other HORECA clients with containers, trays, glasses, wrapping and hygiene products.

In-house manufacturing, pan-India delivery and customised packaging are part of our published catalogue positioning.

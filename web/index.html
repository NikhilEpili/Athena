About us

Packaging partners for food businesses.

Athena Superpack Pvt Ltd is one of India's leading disposable food-packaging companies. Under our brand Zapac, we serve 500+ reseller clients across Maharashtra, with a strong presence in the corporate and HORECA segment across India.

We offer a wide range of high-quality, sustainable and reliable packaging solutions, including plastic and eco-friendly containers, plates, meal trays, glasses, tissues and many more products designed for modern food businesses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make India more sustainable by providing eco-friendly, safe and innovative packaging solutions. We aim to deliver quality, convenience and affordability while reducing environmental impact and supporting the growth of our partners in the food and hospitality industry.

Our Vision

At Athena Superpack Pvt Ltd, our vision is to lead India toward a sustainable future with innovative, eco-friendly food packaging solutions that make everyday consumption greener and smarter.
